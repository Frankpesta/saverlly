How Saverlly works

Saverlly automatically finds and applies coupon codes for you at checkout on supported stores, so you don't have to hunt for discounts yourself.

Saverlly uses affiliate links. When you check out on a supported store after Saverlly applies a code, Saverlly may earn a commission from that store. This does not change the price you pay.

How affiliate links work here

Supported stores participate in affiliate programs. When Saverlly detects you're checking out on one of these stores, it may attribute your visit to Saverlly through that store's affiliate program alongside testing and applying coupon codes. If your purchase completes, the store may pay Saverlly a commission for referring you — the same kind of arrangement used by most coupon and cashback extensions.

What this means for you

You never pay more because of Saverlly's affiliate links, and applying a working coupon code only ever lowers your total. Saverlly does not sell your personal browsing data.

Turning it off

You can uninstall Saverlly at any time from chrome://extensions, or use the "No thanks" option in the popup on any page to skip applying coupons for that visit.
